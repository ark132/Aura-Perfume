How strong?

EDT, EDP or Attar?

The name on the bottle tells you how strong it is and how long it lasts.

EDT

Eau de Toilette

Light and fresh

  • Strength: about 5-15% perfume oil
  • Lasts: around 3-5 hours
  • Feels like: airy, easy, not overpowering
  • Good for: college, daily use, hot days
EDP

Eau de Parfum

Richer and longer lasting

  • Strength: about 15-20% perfume oil
  • Lasts: around 5-8 hours
  • Feels like: deeper, with more presence
  • Good for: office, dates, weddings, evenings
Attar

Attar (Ittar)

Pure oil, no alcohol

  • Strength: concentrated perfume oil
  • Lasts: often 8 hours or more
  • Feels like: warm, close to the skin
  • Good for: festivals, weddings, daily wear in small amounts

Not sure? Start with an EDT for everyday wear, an EDP for special occasions, and an attar if you like a rich, traditional scent. Come to our City Chowk shop and smell all three.
